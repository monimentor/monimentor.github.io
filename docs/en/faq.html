Frequently asked questions about screen time guidance

What is the best approach for screen time guidance with young children?

The most effective approach combines soft boundaries with room for self-discovery. Instead of strict time limits, children learn through Monimentor to feel when enough is enough — a skill they'll carry for life. Monimentor uses visual energy marbles and a digital guide (Moni) to gently guide children toward self-regulation.

At what age can I start conscious screen time guidance?

The earlier, the better. Habits are primarily formed before the age of seven. Monimentor is specifically designed for children aged 4 and up, so you can lay the foundation before their own phone comes into the picture.

Does Monimentor work if my child already has a lot of screen time?

Yes. Monimentor is not meant to punish, but to gradually build awareness. With the energy marbles and Moni as a visual guide, your child will learn to self-dose step by step — regardless of the starting point. The system works with soft boundaries instead of hard limits, so children learn without conflict.

What devices is Monimentor available on?

Currently, Monimentor is only available for Apple devices: iPad, iPhone, and Mac. We are working on an Android version, but it is not yet released. You can link multiple devices to your family account.

Is Monimentor really free and what happens to our data?

Yes, Monimentor is completely free to use. We keep your data private and will never sell or commercialize it to third parties. See our privacy policy for more information. In the future, we may add additional services or features that require a paid subscription.

How is my child's privacy protected, and are screenshots taken?

Monimentor strictly does not take continuous screenshots of your child's screen. We fully respect children's privacy and family life: we only monitor which apps are used and for how long, never personal chats, photos, search history, or private content. All usage statistics are securely encrypted and strictly visible only to your family on the parent dashboard. We never sell data to third parties and display no ads.

Under which name should I create an account?

Always create your account under your own name as a parent or caregiver, not under your child's name. This way, you maintain full control of the account. From your dashboard, you can easily add family members — each family member gets their own profile, keeping things organized. Many parents accidentally create an account under their child's name, but then as a parent you have less overview and fewer management options.

Can I set a device as shared or personal?

When you add a device to your account, you can choose whether it is a shared or personal device. A **personal device** is ideal if you occasionally give your own phone to your child — you will always be logged into Monimentor, unless you manually switch to a child profile in the app. A **shared device** is perfect for a shared family tablet: the app will always ask who is using the device, which is much easier when multiple family members share the same device.

How do I add a device to my account?

Very simple! Follow these steps: 1) Download the Monimentor app on the device, 2) Log in as a parent, 3) The device is automatically added to your account. Done!

How do I add a family member?

Log in to https://my.monimentor.com/ and go to the **Family** menu, then click the **Add** button. All you need to fill in is: name, date of birth, language, PIN code, and the maximum screen time factor. Once added, all devices are ready to use!

My child has forgotten their PIN code

Go to my.monimentor.com and click on **Family** in the menu. Click the three dots next to your child's name and you can reset their PIN code.

What does the AI icon on some images on the Monimentor website mean?

Some images on our website were generated using Artificial Intelligence (AI). Under European law (EU AI Act), we are required to make AI-generated content identifiable. The icon — an AI within a circle — indicates that the image was fully created by AI. We believe in transparency: you should always know exactly what you're seeing. Learn more: EU AI Act transparency requirements. The extent to which we use AI varies: some images are fully AI-generated, while others have been edited or adapted with AI assistance.

Why does Monimentor use AI for images?

We use AI to quickly and cost-effectively create visual content that aligns with our vision. For a free, non-profit app that helps parents with screen time guidance, AI enables us to achieve more with fewer resources. Every AI image is always reviewed and edited by our team — we use AI as a tool, not as a replacement for human judgment and creativity.

Can I tell which images are AI-generated and which are not?

Yes. Wherever an AI-generated image appears, you'll see the AI icon (a circle with the letters 'AI'). It is usually placed in the bottom-right corner of the image, semi-transparent so it's noticeable but not distracting. Images without this icon are hand-made or photographed.

Are the app screens or logo also generated with AI?

No. Our app screens, the Monimentor logo, and all key brand elements are hand-designed by our team. AI is only used for certain illustrations on the marketing website — not for core products like the app or brand identity.

What exactly can I see on the parent dashboard?

Your dashboard gives you a complete overview: a weekly chart showing usage per child, a daily breakdown with minutes per app, and detailed sessions (who, where, when, which apps). You can also see how much of the energy jar is used up. All data is updated in real-time.

How does Monimentor know which apps my child is using?

The Monimentor app on the iPad/iPhone creates a secure VPN tunnel. This allows all network traffic from the device to be tracked — so we know which apps and websites are used, without storing any personal content or data.

What happens when my child reaches their limit?

Your child receives a push notification and Moni reacts visually — for example, becoming tired or sleepy. The energy marbles are full, but there is no hard cut-off. The goal is to teach self-stopping, not to be stopped. If they continue anyway, the energy is deducted from tomorrow's budget.

What is the difference between 'soft boundaries' and a hard screen time limit?

With a hard limit, the screen simply turns off — often leading to conflict. With Monimentor, your child sees visually (energy marbles) and learns to feel when enough is enough. Moni also reacts, but the child learns to stop on their own. This builds self-regulation — a skill they will carry for life.

Does Monimentor work without internet?

No, the VPN tunnel and tracking require an active internet connection. Without internet, Moni cannot update and usage is not tracked. A good WiFi connection is required.

If my child has multiple devices, is the usage combined?

Yes! If you link multiple devices to one child, the total usage across all those devices is counted together. This way you always have a complete overview, no matter how many devices your child uses.

How often is usage updated?

Real-time. Every network transaction is sent directly to the dashboard. So you can see almost instantly which apps your child is using and for how long.

What happens if the VPN connection drops?

Moni receives a signal and the device automatically tries to reconnect. If that doesn't work, the child can manually restart the VPN. In case of a temporary outage, the system will try to deliver the collected information at a later moment — in some cases, usage is then shown retrospectively. If reconnection is not possible, usage during the outage is, of course, not tracked.

Can I set when my child receives notifications?

You can activate or deactivate push notifications in the dashboard settings. The timing of the notifications is set automatically — your child will receive a notification when the energy jar is nearly full or the limit is almost reached. You cannot adjust this timing manually.
