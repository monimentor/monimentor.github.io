Cloud Surfers: Bring the digital world to the kitchen table

The board game that makes screen time open, easy to talk about, and fun. No arguments or secret monitoring, but playing, laughing, and agreeing together.

Why Cloud Surfers

From screen time to a shared family moment

No finger-wagging or strict lectures, but playing and discovering together.

📸

100% Real Content from Your Family

No fictional quiz cards or generic questions. The game revolves around the real, current online interests of your child and family.

🌱

Grows with Your Child

Because your child's online interests change continually, the game naturally changes with them. It stays engaging from ages 4 to 12.

How to Play

How Cloud Surfers works

1📱

Collecting Screenshots

Your child takes playful screenshots via in-app prompts, like 'Your favorite app this week' or 'Something that made you laugh'.

2🎲

Playing at the Table

At the dinner table, those screenshots become the core of the board game. With spinners and prompts, you unravel each other's digital world.

3💬

Funny Conversations

Not an interrogation, but hilarious mini-challenges and lighthearted questions. You genuinely learn what captivates your child online.

4✉️

The Fridge Souvenir

At the end, parent and child fill in a reflection postcard together. A cheerful reminder of your agreed screen habits for the fridge door.

The Origin

TU Delft Research & Handmade Prototypes

To bridge the app and the physical table, TU Delft master's researcher Klaske studied family dynamics for Monimentor. Early prototypes were crafted locally in the workshop of founder Maarten Nijhuis and playtested with real families.
