Digital parenting: 5 handy tips

Maarten

Co-founder

Why digital parenting is important

Screens are everywhere. For children, the digital world is just as real as the physical world. Instead of setting hard limits, it's important to explore together.

Tip 1: Start at an early age

Habits—both healthy and unhealthy—are mostly formed at an early age (<7 years). The ideal time to start is before your child gets their own phone.

Tip 2: Be a guide, not a police officer

Banning often leads to secretive behavior. By keeping the conversation going and playing together, you build trust.

Tip 3: Use soft boundaries

Let children experience what time means. A visual timer (like energy marbles) helps better than an abrupt stop.

Tip 4: Playful learning

Don't make screen time a reward or punishment, but a normal part of the day. This is how children learn self-regulation.

Tip 5: Evaluate together

Discuss what they watched or played. This strengthens family connections and gives you insight into their digital world.
