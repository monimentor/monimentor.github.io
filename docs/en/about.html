About Monimentor

Digital parenting is important and best started at a young age

The Story of Two Fathers

Built by fathers — for our own children, and yours.

Bas&Maarten

We are Bas and Maarten — fathers, software builders, and longtime study friends from Delft. Just like in any household, we saw it unfold with our own kids: tension when the tablet has to go away, the magnetic pull of screens, and the search for balance. We didn't want to be referees who only yell 'no' or remain passive bystanders. We wanted a guide that sparks curiosity and self-regulation — well before the first smartphone arrives.

👨‍👧‍👦Parents of children ages 4 to 12

Our Solution

Soft boundaries for learning through trial and error

Set up Monimentor within 5 minutes. Then we give children soft boundaries in a playful way — just like they learn in the real world.

A bridge to open conversation

Moni guides children in their screen usage, in a playful and age-appropriate way. It's not a replacement for parents, but a supportive companion and conversation starter in family connection.

Our Core Values

Four pillars that guide us.

about.waarden.cards.0.number. Psychology Before Technology

We understand child developmental behavior first, then build. Technology is the means, never the end. Our approach rests on TU Delft scientific research, Netwerk Mediawijsheid expertise, and pedagogical advisor feedback.

about.waarden.cards.1.number. Autonomy Before Restriction

Children learn to steer, not to be told. Self-regulation is the goal, not external control.

about.waarden.cards.2.number. Connection Before Isolation

Screen use is a conversation starter, not a wall. Monimentor connects parents and children.

about.waarden.cards.3.number. Discovery Before Judgment

Usage is not good or bad — it's fodder for conversations and for learning. Every screen moment tells a story.

Scientific Foundation & Partners

Grounded in research, vetted by experts

Guiding screen habits requires more than a simple timer. That's why we build on empirical science and practical expertise.

TU Delft Research

Researcher Klaske conducted extensive graduation research at TU Delft (Industrial Design) for Monimentor into family dynamics, self-regulation, and the role of tangible and visual metaphors in screen time.

Netwerk Mediawijsheid

As an affiliated network partner, we align our pedagogical approach with national standards for media literacy and digital wellbeing.

Pedagogues & Media Coaches

We review our interaction design and soft boundaries with certified media coaches, ensuring they match children's cognitive and emotional stages.

Business Model & Data Ethics

Why free, and how is your data protected?

Parents rightly ask us: 'If it's free, how do you make money?' We believe in complete transparency.

🌱

Why 100% free for families?

Every child deserves a healthy start with digital technology, regardless of family budget. Bas and Maarten build Monimentor on a social mission: the core app and parent dashboard will always remain free.

🛡️

No ads, no sale of children's data

We strictly never sell data to third parties, create no commercial profiles, and show zero advertisements. No screenshots of chats, searches, or photos are ever taken. All data is encrypted and strictly stays within your family.

🚀

How does Monimentor sustain itself?

We build sustainable funding through partnerships with schools and municipalities, sales of our physical family board game Cloud Surfers, and potential future optional premium family extensions — without ever locking the core behind a paywall.
