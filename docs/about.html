About Monimentor

Digital parenting is important and best started at a young age

Our Story

Technology should give — not take away.

Monimentor was born from a deeply held belief: technology should give to children — not take away. Wonder about how tech works. The free will to make choices. Skills and resilience to enter that world with confidence.

Our Solution

Soft boundaries for learning through trial and error

Set up Monimentor in two clicks. Then we give children soft boundaries in a playful way — just like they learn in the real world.

The Conversational Bridge

Moni guides children in their screen usage, in a playful and age-appropriate way. It's not a replacement for the caregiver, but a buffer and companion in the connection.

Our Core Values

Four pillars that guide us.

1. Psychologie vóór Technologie — We begrijpen eerst het gedrag, bouwen daarna.
2. Autonomie vóór Restrictie — Kinderen leren sturen, niet gehoord worden.
3. Verbinding vóór Isolatie — Schermgebruik is een gespreksopener, geen muur.
4. Ontdekking vóór Beoordeling — Gebruik is niet goed of slecht — het is voer voor gesprekken.

Our Team

Maarten and Bas — old study friends from Delft.

We share a passion for technology — developed by having the space as children to wonder about technology and computers. We have years of experience in product development and software engineering. And as fathers, we saw: the world has changed. More complex. More skills and resilience needed to confidently enter the wonderful world of technology.

Building Digital Resilience Together

Digital resilience is the newest form of resilience. And you don't learn it by forbidding. You learn it by practicing.

With Monimentor.
